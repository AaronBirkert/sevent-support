How to Delete Your Account

Follow the steps below to permanently remove your sEvent account and data.

Before You Begin

Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. All of your events, RSVPs, and profile information will be removed. Make sure you have saved any information you'd like to keep.

!!! At the current moment, account deletion is permanent and not reversible !!!
Be sure you want to proceed before continuing.

Note: This is placeholder content. The exact steps will be updated once the account deletion flow is finalized in the app.

Steps to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the sEvent app and sign in to your account.
  2. Tap your profile icon in the top-right corner.
  3. Go to Settings.
  4. Scroll down and tap Account.
  5. Select Delete Account.
  6. Review the information shown, then confirm by tapping Delete.
