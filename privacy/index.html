Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-07

sEvent ("we", "our", "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal data when you use the sEvent mobile application.

1. Data We Collect

We may collect the following categories of data:

  • Account data: email address, username, and authentication identifiers.
  • Event data: events, pages, cards, guest lists, responses, and related content you create or share.
  • Device and app data: app version, device type, operating system, and basic diagnostic information.
  • Notification data: device push token, notification preferences, and delivery metadata when notifications are enabled.

2. How We Use Data

We use data to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Provide core app features (event management, collaboration, and invitations).
  • Sync your data across devices.
  • Send event-related notifications through Firebase Cloud Messaging and platform push services when enabled by you.
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and improve reliability.

3. Legal Basis (EEA/UK Users)

Where applicable, we process data based on:

  • Contract: to provide the app features you request.
  • Legitimate interests: security, fraud prevention, and service improvement.
  • Consent: for notifications and other optional features where required.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data.

We may share data with service providers only as needed to operate the app, such as:

  • Supabase (authentication, database, storage, and backend services).
  • Firebase (cloud messaging infrastructure for push notifications).
  • Infrastructure/hosting providers.
  • Push notification providers (Apple/Google) when notifications are enabled.

These providers process data under contractual and security obligations.

5. Data Retention

We keep personal data only as long as necessary to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

If you request account deletion from within the app or through a support request, we will delete or anonymize personal data unless retention is legally required.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access your personal data.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your data.
  • Restrict or object to processing.
  • Data portability.

To make a request, contact us using the details below.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect data. However, no system is completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

sEvent is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children without appropriate consent.

9. International Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own, where our providers operate. We take appropriate steps to protect transferred data.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date when changes are made.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact:
